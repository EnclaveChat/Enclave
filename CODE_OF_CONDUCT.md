# Code of conduct

Enclave is built by people from many countries, many of whom face real risks
for speaking freely. Treat everyone here with respect.

* Be kind and assume good faith. Critique ideas and code, not people.
* No harassment, threats, doxxing or discrimination of any kind.
* Never ask anyone to reveal who they are, where they are, or why they need
  privacy.
* Keep discussion on topic and in public channels unless privacy is needed.

Report problems to conduct@enclave.chat (to be activated before the first
public release). Maintainers will respond, may remove content, and may ban
people who break these rules. Reporters are protected from retaliation.
